Zero Down

Solo Developer

Zero Down is a top-down gallery shooter set during the Battle of Midway. The player pilots an American aircraft tasked with shooting down waves of incoming Japanese planes while dodging enemy fire. I designed and implemented core gameplay systems, including smooth player controls, bullet mechanics, and enemy behavior.
