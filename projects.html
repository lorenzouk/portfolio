Blind Polar Winds

Project Manager & Gameplay Programmer
10 Weeks ⋅ Team of 3

Overview

Blind Polar Winds is a modification for Nexus Games' Polar Winds. In this game, you connect each anchor points with one another by working with two other players to score as much points as possible before the timer runs out. In our modification, we blinded all players to their own colors and added rifts that give buffs or debuffs.

Contributions

  • Implemented rift mechanics that give buffs or debuffs
  • Managed the project and coordinated development efforts
  • Overhauled and designed the UI/UX by modifying the existing interface

Bomb Golf

Gameplay & Systems Developer
1 Week ⋅ Team of 5

Overview

Bomb Golf is an interactive 3D golf game with a high stakes twist. Players have a limited number of attempts to sink the bomb into the hole. Failure results in an explosion. At the start, players collect turns, adding a strategic layer that makes the game more manageable. This project focuses on 3D rendering, game patterns and team-based development.

Contributions

  • Developed golf mechanic and physics simulation
  • Added VFX to bomb and designed golf course layout
  • Refactored code for improved performance and maintainability
  • Designed concept and logic of the game and mechanics
